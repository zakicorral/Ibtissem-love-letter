For the girl who means more than these words can hold

My love,

I wanted to make you something instead of simply sending you another message. Something you could open whenever you wanted and remember that, somewhere in this world, there is someone who chose to put his feelings into every little detail just for you.

There are so many moments with you that I could write about. The funny ones. The stupid ones. The quiet ones. The moments where we said almost nothing and somehow it still felt like enough.

What I love most is not one perfect moment. It is us — all the little pieces that became something I never want to take for granted.

Thank you for being part of my life, for the memories we have already made, and for all the ones we have not lived yet.

If I could give you one thing through this screen, it would be the feeling behind these words: you are deeply loved.

So keep this little corner of the internet. Come back to it on a good day, a bad day, or one of those ordinary days when you just need a reminder.

I love you, Ibtissem.
More than a page could ever explain.

Always yours, Zaki
